Tajweed learning guide

A quick introduction to common recitation rules.

Ghunnah

A nasal sound, typically held for two counts in its relevant rules, such as noon or meem with shaddah.

Madd

Elongation of a vowel sound. Its duration depends on the type of madd and the recitation context.

Qalqalah

A slight echoing or bounce on the letters ق ط ب ج د when the rule applies, especially in a state of sukoon.
